## v3.1 acceptance rule

The six new implementation rows and the T12 clarification must be updated to
`PASS` only after code, tests, Evidence and Bound Coverage are present. Any
retry, hook rejection, consolidation schedule/retry or handoff wakeup must
have a deterministic budget and a persisted or replayable decision. A real
Provider/Worker blocker remains a blocker; a local adapter or synthetic result
cannot promote it.
